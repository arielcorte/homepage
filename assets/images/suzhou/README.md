# Historical Suzhou banners

These are scans of human-made historical woodblock prints, not generated images.

**Westlake Panorama (西湖全景)**, mid-1700s. Southeast China, Suzhou,
Qing dynasty, Qianlong reign (1736–95). Artist not identified in the museum record.
Woodblock print, ink on paper, printed with three woodblocks in shades of black
on four sheets. Depicts West Lake in Hangzhou; the prints were made in Suzhou.

Collection: Cleveland Museum of Art. Credit: Purchase from the J. H. Wade Fund.

Downloaded from the museum's print-resolution image URLs:
`https://openaccess-cdn.clevelandart.org/{accession}/{accession}_print.jpg`.
Hugo produces compressed WebP derivatives. Every banner uses a single sheet,
cropped inside its printed border to exclude paper edges and assembly seams,
then cropped to banner proportions. Original scans are retained here, without recoloring,
retouching, or generated additions. CSS crops further to fit mobile screens.
