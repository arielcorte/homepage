I'm Ariel Corte. I love to build together.

I build stuff not only because it is cool and fun but because it is my way to contribute to my community.

I started electronics at my high school. I chose that degree because I wanted to understand electronic boards.

Right before the pandemic, I got interested in software. Started with Arduino which was closer to electronics but quickly migrated to web development. Web development felt powerful. You could code something and **instantly have it accessible for everyone around the globe**.

{{< timeline >}}

I found Robotic Crew when I saw the offer looking for a software engineer with knowledge in electronics and great energy. It also had to be local to my city. **That post felt like it was written for me.**

At my last project I built several internal tools to help the team work faster. You start thinking as a team and **where can I put my effort so their effort is more efficient.**

I like the community feeling and having a voice. Those are things I'm developing in the background. You would say I'm **quite chatty for the typical engineer profile** and I'm comfortable with it. My Instagram can show a lot of content but it's more personal and funnier. I want to use this corner for more structured writing. Without losing humor of course.
